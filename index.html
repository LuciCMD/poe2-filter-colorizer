How It Works

Every rule in a NeverSink filter ends its comment with a style class, such as !currency_a or !uniques_b. The themer looks that class up in the theme and rewrites only the colour lines the rule already has: text, border, background, light beam and minimap icon colour.

It never adds, removes or reorders a condition, so the items you see are exactly the ones your FilterBlade settings chose. After every conversion it compares the items shown and hidden before and after, and warns you if anything moved. Minimap icon shapes and sizes, and temporary beams, are kept from your file.

A style class the theme doesn't know, for example from a new league, is left at its original colour and listed in the report.

Works with NeverSink's PoE 2 filters from FilterBlade. Source and Theme Guide on GitHub When you download, the page adds one to a public counter. Nothing about you or your filter is sent.
